🎓 How to use SOS Beat Lab — step-by-step tutorial

Start with a rhythm, write a melody, sculpt a synth, then render and arrange your finished sound. Each pattern now has 32 steps (two 4/4 bars). All original sounds remain available.

1. Build a beat

Open Step Sequencer. Click a tile to add a drum hit. Drag across tiles to paint; begin on a filled tile to erase. Choose a sound for each row.

2. Add a melody

Open Melody Roll. Click or drag over note tiles to add notes; start on a filled note and drag to erase. Your notes belong to the currently selected pattern.

3. Shape your synth

Open Synth Forge. Choose a patch, preview C4, adjust oscillators and reverb. Drag the glowing dot horizontally for cutoff and vertically for resonance. Melody Roll notes use these synth settings.

4. Mix & add effects

Open Mixer & FX to adjust gain, pan and EQ for each drum track. The Lead channel also shapes Melody Roll output.

5. Arrange patterns

Use Add Pattern to make more sections. Select a pattern to edit its drums and melody. Check patterns in the left panel to play them in sequence.

6. Record & export

Set BPM and song length, then use the recording or export tools. Download WAV or project JSON to your computer; use JSON to reopen projects later.

7. Longer patterns

Each sequencer pattern now contains 32 steps (two bars). Use the horizontal scroll area on smaller screens. Older 16-step projects expand automatically when loaded.

8. Export a synth stem

Open Synth Forge, choose a preset and room, then choose a pitch. Export Full Synth WAV keeps the note's release and reverb tail. Render & Place also assigns that WAV to a selected sequencer row and activates step one.

9. Arrange and download

Select patterns on the left, set song length, then use Play or Render WAV. Each 32-step pattern lasts two bars. WAV downloads are saved by your browser; keep imported samples for later sessions.
