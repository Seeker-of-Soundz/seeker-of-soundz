Producer Hub 4.0

Guided production tutorial

1

Load your project

Upload Video 1, add more camera angles or sequential clips, then upload licensed music. The timeline appears directly beneath the transport controls.

2

Timeline and advanced tools

Play, pause, rewind, and forward above the timeline. Edit Mode, Select, Razor, Trim, and Snap sit directly beneath the timeline. Open Advanced Timeline Tools only when you need Timed Effects, Auto-Sync, or Cut & Edit.

3

Direct video range sliders

The left and right handles on every video clip are always active—you do not need to select Trim first. Drag the left edge to choose the first playable frame and the right edge to choose the last. The preview follows the handle while dragging, and footage outside the selected range is excluded from playback and export.

4

AI Camera Director

Analyze beats, choose a director style and intensity, then generate camera motion, optional keyframes, and multicamera switches across the project.

5

GPU Timeline cache

GPU Cache reduces timeline layout and paint work and uses OffscreenCanvas support when available. Browser hardware acceleration should remain enabled, although the browser controls the final GPU/CPU path.

6

Professional keyframes

Add keyframes at the playhead for camera scale, rotation, position, original text movement, opacity, and music volume. Select a keyframe to edit or remove it.

7

Curves editor

Apply Linear, Ease, Bounce, Elastic, or Cubic interpolation to selected keyframes or the entire animation pass.

8

Direct music sliders and offset

Use the two handles directly on the waveform to choose the audible song range. The Music Offset controls now sit below the lane instead of covering the waveform. Playback starts from the left handle after the chosen offset, stays audible through the selected range, and stops at the right handle.

9

Multicamera editing

Use the camera grid or Camera 1–4 buttons during playback. Angle changes can be recorded as timeline cuts, and AI Director can generate them from drops.

10

Save and continue later

Download a settings preset or a full .sosproject file. Portable projects may embed local video and audio but can become very large; settings-only projects require re-linking media after import.

11

YouTube tools and authorized downloads

Use the YouTube search and preview tools to find references, copy a URL, and open the external converter module. Download only videos you own, have permission to use, or whose license allows downloading and editing. Third-party converter sites may contain intrusive advertising; a reputable browser content blocker can reduce pop-ups, but always review the site and never install unexpected software.

12

Safe SOS project files

Use the SOS Project Files bar beneath the transport controls to save or import .sosproject and .sospreset files. Settings-only saves are lightweight and safest. Portable media is embedded only when the selected files fit the browser’s memory-safe limit; otherwise Producer Hub automatically downloads a settings-only project and asks you to re-link the original media later.

13

Studio+ professional tools

Open Inspector → Studio+ for working track locks, mute and solo, selected-clip colors, compound and nested sequences, adjustment layers, blend modes, color controls, masks, audio processing, scene and song analysis, proxy mode, recovery autosave, asset collections, export presets, and local plugin manifests. Collaboration is intentionally excluded.

14

Render and download

Use Render Focus for a cleaner export workspace. Keep hardware acceleration enabled and close unnecessary tabs. The processed canvas, keyframes, camera direction, effects, audio ducking, and layers are included in the render.
