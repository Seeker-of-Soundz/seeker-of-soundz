# v7.0.76 - SOS Beat Lab WAV Sample Kit

Added eight bundled WAV audio files already present in the website assets as selectable, decoded sample instruments in the Beat Lab sound browser. Existing 256 procedural sound variations remain available. Samples are playable in the step sequencer, preview, and offline WAV render, and sample IDs persist in JSON project data. Browser-supported imported samples remain session-only. These are pre-existing audio assets; their original recording provenance has not been independently verified.
